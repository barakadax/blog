## What is a standards organization

A standards organization is an organization that develops and publishes technical standards for products, services, and processes.

## Why is it important to have standards organizations

Standards organizations are important because they help to ensure that products and services are safe, reliable, and compatible with each other.
They help to promote innovation and competition by providing a level playing field for companies and ensuring that different products can work together (interoperability).

## Types of standards organizations

Standards organizations can be categorized by their scope and nature:

- **International:** Develop standards for global use.
- **Regional:** Focus on specific geographical areas.
- **National:** Develop and manage standards for a specific country.
- **Industry Consortia / SSOs:** Formed by companies to quickly develop standards for emerging technologies.
- **Professional and Scientific:** Organizations representing professional fields that set technical standards.
- **Governmental / Regulatory:** Agencies that create or adopt standards as part of law or policy.
- **De facto:** Developed by industry players to ensure compatibility and interoperability.
- **Open source communities:** Developed by the community to ensure compatibility and interoperability.
- **Academic:** Developed by academic institutions to ensure compatibility and interoperability.

## Examples of standards organizations

Here is an example for each of the types listed above:

- **International:** **ISO** (International Organization for Standardization)
    - **Famous standard:** **ISO 9001** (Quality Management Systems)
- **Regional:** **ETSI** (European Telecommunications Standards Institute)
    - **Famous standard:** **GSM** (Global System for Mobile Communications)
- **National:** **ANSI** (American National Standards Institute)
    - **Famous standard:** **ASCII** (American Standard Code for Information Interchange)
- **Industry Consortia / SSOs:** **W3C** (World Wide Web Consortium)
    - **Famous standard:** **HTML5** (Hypertext Markup Language)
- **Professional and Scientific:** **IEEE** (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers)
    - **Famous standard:** **IEEE 802.11** (Wi-Fi)
- **Governmental / Regulatory:** **NIST** (National Institute of Standards and Technology)
    - **Famous standard:** **AES** (Advanced Encryption Standard)
- **De facto:** **Adobe**
    - **Famous standard:** **PDF** (Portable Document Format) — originally proprietary to Adobe; later released as an open standard and published as ISO 32000-1 in 2008.
- **Open source communities:** **Linux Foundation**
    - **Famous standard:** **SPDX** (Software Package Data Exchange)
- **Academic:** **ISTE** (International Society for Technology in Education)
    - **Famous standard:** **ISTE Standards** (Framework for using technology in education)
