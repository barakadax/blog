## First-Person Perspectives

### First-Person
The camera is positioned directly through the eyes of the player character, providing the most immersive sense of "being" in the game world.
![DOOM](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/2492270746255816671/383703DA85780A29211E171347D72A11779D2B4F/?imw=1920&&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Call Of Duty](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/16780821962861043764/720388605DDE74F796AC15DC0C25493EA5D9E4F2/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![CS:GO](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/263836971406425643/CAEE8EBB28C1B32D3CF5C88B40C977E96286D463/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
- **Examples**: Doom, Call of Duty, CS:GO

### VR First-Person
A specialized first-person view for Virtual Reality headsets that tracks the player's head movements and often provides independent control of their hands.
![Half-Life Alyx](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/35568576812748848/C808B1A2AE7062FC42542518F66ADF60F46B41F6/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Resident Evil VR](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/cjDHtdNEeWcbvhykZehYn7.png)
![DOOM VFR](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/54714481517794042/53A0AB1891AFF622A81B7B77D35DFEC868AC2686/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
- **Examples**: Half-Life: Alyx, Resident Evil VR, Doom VFR

### First-Person Simulation
Focuses on technical realism and high-fidelity cockpits, often used in vehicles where every switch and gauge is interactive.
![Microsoft Flight Simulator](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/2040735491764380011/5DF642D60E26D91BDE2A23431834A15ABBBA9039/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Euro Truck Simulator 2](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/11655387156413872235/E6C17A4FEF4CFD6ECC278BD9716F612F699AA38C/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Farming Simulator 22](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/11241259181967323441/1F42B7E4298E5ACEFEBB8F6712E1A3A678944921/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
- **Examples**: Microsoft Flight Simulator, Euro Truck Simulator 2, Farming Simulator 22

## Third-Person Perspectives

### Over-the-Shoulder
A close-up third-person view where the camera rests just behind the character's shoulder, balancing character visibility with a clear view of what's ahead.
![Resident Evil 4 Remake](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/10974104769229521824/7D422A0CF2C3A07844D049DA2B3435D81934D9C9/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![God of War](https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/GoW_04-02-18.jpg)
![The Last of Us](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/31070583478558400/3860B4F3B95E3D94AE0BBC57581D595BB702F5CE/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
- **Examples**: Resident Evil 4 Remake, God of War (2018), The Last of Us

### Third-Person
The camera follows the character from a distance, allowing the player to see the character's full body and the surrounding environment simultaneously.
![Grand Theft Auto V](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/13123319966724823417/1606808FD114B6D90D133F194B829A869544A185/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Dark Souls](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/25426501416171648/6A4ED60A01442A131E7F97382604A0CAB09C9A62/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Horizon Zero Dawn](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/44572507903931687/9E15D5FC76A7041BE00813335D4045C4C47A1D97/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
- **Examples**: Grand Theft Auto V, Dark Souls, Horizon Zero Dawn

### Behind-the-Back
A fixed camera angle that stays strictly locked behind the character, common in linear platformers and racing games.
![Crash Bandicoot](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/15797465595280791014/82F2F96806293DB533E75429D6EB1B561D39F8FC/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Skyroads](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/he/5/56/Skyroads.jpg)
![Megabonk](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/11341790199637484655/2D351EC6558666A1425C4E617EFA0A0B472481A3/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
- **Examples**: Crash Bandicoot, Skyroads, Megabonk

### Fixed / Cinematic
Uses pre-set camera angles that don't follow the character dynamically, often creating a movie-like atmosphere and tension.
![ God of war](https://www.gamespot.com/a/uploads/original/416/4161502/2904228-gow3%20-%20gow3%20-%202015-07-08%2015-49-1907.jpg)
![Resident Evil](https://static0.polygonimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/chorus/uploads/chorus_asset/file/25505991/RE1_13.jpeg)
![The Medium](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/13742301503993339127/138A6B19B4C105123A52BD57A431D1113CF28057/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
- **Examples**: God of war (2005), Resident Evil (1996), The Medium

## 2D & Side-Scrolling

### 2D Side-Scroller
The gameplay and visuals are purely two-dimensional, with the camera moving horizontally as the character traverses the level.
![Captain Claw](https://captainclaw.net/img/fun/alpha42.gif)
![Cuphead](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/18368739870050283508/E61A761BF7C0629D122CE71C25380262782ADB89/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Hollow Knight](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/11150526267167504614/24877CE94C544A94FD183F24929D34DE2B5F3FEA/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
- **Examples**: Captain Claw, Cuphead, Hollow Knight

### 2.5D Side-Scroller
Combines 3D graphics and environments with a restricted 2D gameplay plane, creating a sense of depth while maintaining classic mechanics.
![Metroid Dread](https://static0.thegamerimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/scaifa.jpg)
![Chicken Invaders](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/18309870057080560353/979E7EE9164D062AAFF20AC7B1AA21AC89329DE7/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Little Nightmares](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/15297536206718279/07252CA4ABF8336891476BF29B1049DE5F05B80E/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
- **Examples**: Metroid Dread, Chicken Invaders, Little Nightmares

### Side-View / Profile
A cross-section view of a location, often used in management or building games to show multiple floors or rooms at once.
![Fallout Shelter](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/9754107273366553699/884FDA25FAF6632137D8F67A2A6E3939A923E20F/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Terraria](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/13084119356079161019/2DB5DEAB3B982CE5AB137553F3F5C518F2F07CAD/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![This War of Mine](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/13469782396973730277/5D197058AD710983A02AD99E5F636535AF8FF600/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
- **Examples**: Fallout Shelter, Terraria, This War of Mine

## Top-Down & Isometric

### Isometric / Dimetric
Uses a pseudo-3D orthographic projection (usually at a 3/4 angle) to provide a clear view of the battlefield without the distortion of true perspective.
![Diablo II](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/16431219902727744083/F0D10010AC547290D4E6E507035F53CF92405BA9/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Hades](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/12486105968660685181/D8B9DA4E88D3592F9879DC2285D3D6C9096697A6/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Battle Chasers: Nightwar](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/15524754399842131372/DB102FEA85CCA3E6A319D7B7B8C6E437DD5BB7A2/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
- **Examples**: Diablo II, Hades, Battle Chasers: Nightwar

### Top-Down / Birds-Eye
The camera looks straight down at the action, providing a tactical overview of the character and their surroundings.
![BTD6](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/9677906204267797215/B2525140BE4E6B9CC24F689F5383B9EF7B58310E/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![DOTA 2](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/572273303406416356/7522ADD3B0E4C6399D50BBF29A4FA6FF56D50480/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Cult of the Lamb](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/13458544890342753516/568C7D6754A7165E7D47AD2E3045FA8F151B9DD5/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
- **Examples**: BTD6, DOTA 2, Cult of the Lamb

### Top-Down Scrolling
A top-down view where the world moves past the player (or the player moves through it), typically seen in "shmups" or vertical shooters.
![AirStrike 3D](https://ia801209.us.archive.org/3/items/as3d_demo/air3d.jpg)
![Ikaruga](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/2482137828773906799/47D02DC7381E2547062D01F07D412E677C4A9271/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Spy Hunter](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/1JYuPm-eVOw/hqdefault.jpg)
- **Examples**: AirStrike 3D, Ikaruga, Spy Hunter

## Static & Abstract

### Static / Single-Screen
The entire game occurs within one fixed screen that does not scroll, common in classic arcade games and puzzle titles.
![Pac-Man](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/538523741995303917/096E61FF39EDD076436444056870FAAAB74E0FCE/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Bejeweled 3](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/10270418037040651319/2667199403EB7D99E20F5CAB6D883DD8D55018C1/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Peggle](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/9472559827042607543/7398459161B9BF765A378D98EAB8233903212141/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
- **Examples**: Pac-Man, Bejeweled 3, Peggle

### Visual Novel / Static 2D
Features static or lightly animated character sprites over a background, focusing on storytelling and dialogue choices.
![Doki Doki Literature Club!](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/878624711073022566/8B2D3989EF375267B219494C9CD27D2990F24131/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Steins;Gate](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/412830/ss_56d0a07b2ca57c72eb9c74ca918d49e3cf7a06a6.1920x1080.jpg?t=1715703398)
![Fate/stay night](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/37825433777652391/7AACA87EDB96970ECEE493A0670D5D48958ABA93/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
- **Examples**: Doki Doki Literature Club!, Steins;Gate, Fate/stay night

### Text-Based / Abstract
Relies on textual descriptions or abstract icons rather than a literal camera, leaving the visuals to the player's imagination.
![Zork](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/924834712723998811/19C063A026A164F41FE445115AB1870FEB93CD3D/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Football Manager](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/16777301121248242599/459801161FC9CCEB191990D20B7FDEFE6CAD9B40/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy](https://www.cnet.com/a/img/hub/2014/03/11/82709564-bf2d-11e3-bddd-d4ae52e62bcc/hhguide_1.jpg)
- **Examples**: Zork, Football Manager, Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy

## Unique Perspectives

### Second-Person
A rare and experimental POV where you see your own character through the eyes of a different character or NPC in the scene.
![Driver: San Francisco](https://cdn.neowin.com/news/images/uploaded/Driver-San-Francisco-Multiplayer.jpg)
![Psychonauts](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/13697478408729430518/935F224527E8AF3DA72832D7565DE915EA834BBD/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false)
![Siren](https://bloody-disgusting.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/siren.jpg)
- **Examples**: Driver: San Francisco, Psychonauts, Siren
